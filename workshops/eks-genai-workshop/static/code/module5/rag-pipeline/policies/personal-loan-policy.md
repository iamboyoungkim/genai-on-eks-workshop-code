# Personal Loan Policy

Policy ID: LP-PL-2024-02 | Effective: 2024-02-15 | Owner: Consumer Lending Credit Committee

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy governs unsecured personal loans for general purposes such as debt consolidation, medical expenses, major
purchases and life events. Home improvement requests must use the Home Improvement Loan Policy (LP-HI-2024-03).

## 2. Loan Amount and Term

- Minimum loan amount: $1,000
- Maximum loan amount: $25,000
- Loan terms: 12 to 60 months
- First-time customers are limited to $15,000 until they have 12 months of on-time payments.

## 3. Applicant Eligibility

- Minimum annual gross income: $30,000.
- Minimum continuous employment: 1 year with the current employer, or 2 years in the same field.
- Maximum debt-to-income (DTI) ratio after the new payment: 40%.
- Applicants must be at least 18 years old and have a valid Social Security Number.

## 4. Debt Consolidation

For debt consolidation loans, the proceeds should be paid directly to the creditors being consolidated. The DTI check
uses the obligations that remain after consolidation.

## 5. Prohibited Uses

Personal loans may not be used for gambling, investment in securities, post-secondary tuition, business purposes or
any illegal activity. A stated purpose in this list results in a DECLINE.

## 6. Required Documentation

- Signed application (wet signature or verified electronic signature)
- Government-issued photo ID
- Proof of income (see Income Requirements Policy LP-INC-2024-01)

## 7. Decision Rules

Personal loans use the same risk score thresholds as the Risk Assessment Guidelines (section 5):
80 or higher APPROVE, 60 to 79 CONDITIONAL APPROVAL, below 60 DECLINE.
