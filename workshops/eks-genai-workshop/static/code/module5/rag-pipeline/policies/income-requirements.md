# Income Requirements Policy

Policy ID: LP-INC-2024-01 | Effective: 2024-01-10 | Owner: Credit Risk Management

## 1. Minimum Income by Product

| Product | Minimum annual gross income |
|---|---|
| Personal loan | $30,000 |
| Home improvement loan | $40,000 |

Income below the product minimum results in a DECLINE regardless of other factors.

## 2. Debt-to-Income (DTI) Ratio

DTI = (total monthly debt payments including the new loan payment) / (gross monthly income).

- Personal loans: DTI must be 40% or lower.
- Home improvement loans: DTI must be 43% or lower.
- DTI between 36% and the product limit is acceptable but adds risk points (see Risk Assessment Guidelines).

When the applicant's existing monthly debts are unknown, the underwriter must request them before a final approval.
A conditional approval may be issued with "confirm monthly debt obligations" listed as a condition.

## 3. Income Verification

Stated income must be verified by one of the following:

- two most recent pay stubs plus the most recent W-2, or
- an employment and income verification from an approved verification service, or
- two years of tax returns for self-employed applicants.

## 4. Income Variance Tolerance

Verified income may differ from stated income by up to 10%. If the variance is larger, use the lower verified figure
for all calculations and record the variance in the decision.

## 5. Employment Stability

- Employment must be full-time or permanent part-time.
- Gaps in employment longer than 6 months in the last 2 years require a written explanation.
- Probationary employment (first 90 days with a new employer) is not accepted.
