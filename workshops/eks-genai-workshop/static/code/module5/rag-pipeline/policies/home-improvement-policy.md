# Home Improvement Loan Policy

Policy ID: LP-HI-2024-03 | Effective: 2024-03-01 | Owner: Consumer Lending Credit Committee

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy governs unsecured home improvement loans used for repairs, renovations, energy efficiency upgrades and
accessibility modifications to a residential property. It applies to all applications processed by Loan Buddy and by
human underwriters.

## 2. Loan Amount and Term

- Minimum loan amount: $5,000
- Maximum loan amount: $50,000
- Loan terms: 24 to 84 months
- Loans above $35,000 require a contractor estimate or a written project plan.

## 3. Applicant Eligibility

- Minimum annual gross income: $40,000 (see Income Requirements Policy LP-INC-2024-01).
- Minimum continuous employment with the current employer: 2 years. Self-employed applicants need 2 years of tax returns.
- Maximum debt-to-income (DTI) ratio after the new payment: 43%.
- The loan amount must not exceed 35% of verified annual gross income.

## 4. Property and Residency

### 4.1 Owner-occupied applicants

Applicants who own and occupy the property qualify for the full loan range ($5,000 to $50,000).

### 4.2 Non-owner applicants

Applicants who do not own the property (renting, or living in a property owned by a family member) may apply only when:

- the requested amount is $15,000 or less,
- the property owner gives written consent for the improvement work, and
- residency at the address is proven with a utility bill or lease dated within the last 60 days.

A co-signer who owns the property is recommended and removes the $15,000 cap.

### 4.3 Residency duration

Residency at the current address of less than 12 months is a risk factor (see Risk Assessment Guidelines, section 3).
It does not disqualify an applicant on its own.

## 5. Required Documentation

- Signed application (wet signature or verified electronic signature; a typed name is not accepted as a signature)
- Government-issued photo ID
- Proof of income (see Income Requirements Policy)
- Proof of residency for non-owner applicants (section 4.2)

## 6. Decision Rules

- All eligibility criteria met and risk score 80 or higher: APPROVE.
- All eligibility criteria met and risk score 60 to 79: CONDITIONAL APPROVAL. List every outstanding condition.
- Any eligibility criterion not met, or risk score below 60: DECLINE.
- Every decision must cite the policy sections it relies on.
