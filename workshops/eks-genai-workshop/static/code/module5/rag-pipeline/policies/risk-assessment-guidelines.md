# Risk Assessment Guidelines

Policy ID: LP-RISK-2024-04 | Effective: 2024-04-01 | Owner: Credit Risk Management

## 1. Overview

Every application receives a risk score from 0 to 100, where a higher score means lower risk. The score starts at 100
and points are deducted for each risk factor below. The final decision thresholds are in section 5.

## 2. Document Risk

- Document authenticity score of 85 or higher: no deduction.
- Authenticity score 75 to 84: deduct 5 points.
- Authenticity score below 75: deduct 15 points and require manual review of the document before funding.
- Typed signature instead of a wet or verified electronic signature: deduct 5 points and require a valid signature.

## 3. Residency and Address Risk

- Residency at the current address under 12 months: deduct 10 points.
- Address ownership not verified: deduct 5 points.
- Address fraud score 21 to 50: deduct 5 points. Address fraud score above 50: DECLINE.
- Address flagged for manual review: require proof of residency before funding.

## 4. Income and Employment Risk

- Employment under the product minimum: DECLINE.
- Employment stability score below 70: deduct 10 points.
- DTI between 36% and the product limit: deduct 10 points.
- Income variance above 10%: deduct 5 points.

## 5. Decision Thresholds

| Risk score | Decision |
|---|---|
| 80 to 100 | APPROVE |
| 60 to 79 | CONDITIONAL APPROVAL |
| 0 to 59 | DECLINE |

A conditional approval must list every condition that has to be met before funding, for example identity
verification, a valid signature, proof of residency or confirmed debt obligations.

## 6. Pricing Adjustments

- Risk score 80 or higher: standard rate.
- Risk score 70 to 79: standard rate plus 0.5%.
- Risk score 60 to 69: standard rate plus 1.0%.

## 7. Citations

Credit decisions must name the policy ID and section for every rule that affected the decision, for example
"LP-RISK-2024-04 section 3".
